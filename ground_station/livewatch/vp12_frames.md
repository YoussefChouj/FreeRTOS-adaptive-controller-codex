# vp 12 frame: S10X (S10 + swing ANF + battery sag + motor lag; MULTI build) = exp14 frame, slot 3 carries the vp 12 readbacks and ext weights

Slots 0-2 as exp14 (core loops, MRAC u_ad / u_nom / e, Theta[0..5] + Whatf[0..5] all axes, body accel Acc_X/Y_Real at 50 Hz).
Slot 3 (55 floats, was 60 in exp14, so the budget stays under exp14's 86,720 of 87,552 B/s): 12 labels, vp 12 readbacks
(tracked swing frequency pitch / roll, V latched at arm, requested and applied ext mask per axis) and Theta[6..13] for all
four axes (S10X uses 14 features: S6 + ext 0..7; Z uses the battery slot only). Feature values (phi) are not stored on the
drone: rebuild them offline from Acc_X/Y_Real (slot 2), real_voltage (slot 0) and anf_hz. Pack voltage = real_voltage (slot 0).
