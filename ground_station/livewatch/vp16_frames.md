# vp 16 / 17 / 18 frame: 3L-v2 (L2 composite prediction-error law, D self-tuning gain; pitch and roll only)

Slots 0-1 as vp13 / exp14 (core loops, MRAC u_ad / u_nom / u_def / e / e_dot, Theta[0..5] + Whatf[0..5] all axes, thrust).
Slot 2 keeps exp14's 33 floats but swaps the optical-flow module and RPM channels for the 3L-v2 signals: the L2
prediction error pe, the leaky angle integral e_int and the filtered regressor Phi_f[0..5] for pitch and roll. With
Theta (slots 0-1) adaptive_review rebuilds negd_f = pe + Theta'Phi_f, the part of the plant u_ad has to cancel, and
reports the fit 1 - var(pe)/var(negd_f) per segment (section "3L-v2 prediction error").
Slot 3 (18 floats, 5 Hz): the 12 labels plus the D gain p_gain and the filter outputs u_f, xd_f for pitch and roll.
Budget: at most exp14's 86,720 of 87,552 B/s. Counting 4 B per value (the exp14 convention) slot 2 is unchanged and slot 3
saves 42 x 4 B x 10 Hz nominal = 1,680 B/s. Counting real sizes, the 16 swapped-out channels were int8/int16 (32 B), so
slot 2 grows by 32 B x 50 Hz = 1,600 B/s, still under the 1,680 B/s slot 3 saves.

Needs an ELF built from f00412a or later (Phi_f, pe, p_gain, u_f, xd_f do not exist before it). Rows 16-18 run in
both the STRUCT6 and the MULTI build (basis 0). For optical-flow truth use vp13_frames.md instead.

python -m ground_station.livewatch.stream_log --frames ground_station/livewatch/vp16_frames.md --seconds 3600 --out logs/vp16_<name>.csv
