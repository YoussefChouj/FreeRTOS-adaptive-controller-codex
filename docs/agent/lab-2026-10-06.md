## Asymmetric load (PROPOSED)

Steady roll/pitch torque is carried by the rate-loop integrator (gyrox/gyroy Ui cap 160 CCR) plus rate Kp 5 x the
attitude integrator (cap 26), up to 290 CCR of per-motor differential. That is about +-2 N per motor at the bench
curve slope near hover, enough for a few hundred grams a few centimetres off-centre. Arm length is not recorded,
so this has to be checked in flight. Expect a tilt toward the load and a sideways drift during takeoff while the
integrators charge. Start with the payload half-way out, then move it further. The abort monitor trips at 35 deg
tilt held 0.2 s. The firmware TILT kill trips at 60 deg (WP-42 finding: 45 deg PROPOSED).

If the drone stops answering both the sticks and the kill switch, assume a firmware fault: the fault handler spins
and TIM3 keeps the last motor PWM, and the watchdog is off. Cut battery power; no software failsafe will act
(docs/firmware-safety.md, "Fault in flight").

## Risks to watch in each debrief

- Corners: an unrounded 90 deg corner at 0.3 m/s is a 0.42 m/s reference velocity step (geometry). Both paths
  round the corners (corner_cut 1 and 2) and keep the speed low. `time_profile` does not slow down at corners.
- Altitude sag under payload: see the table.
- Loop outputs pinned (Z_rate, gyrox/gyroy, gyroz U in telemetry): the yaw angle Ui cap is only 2, so an asymmetric
  load that adds yaw torque relies on the gyroz loop (Ui cap 500).
- New firmware: the first HEAD flight is the shakedown. Any surprise there stops the payload set.
- Not ready: no flight data for the `waypoints` shape. Pass bands are PROPOSED until the first debrief.
