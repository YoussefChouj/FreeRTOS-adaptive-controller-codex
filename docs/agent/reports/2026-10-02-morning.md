## 1. What was learned (evidence: `docs/agent/reports/WP-13.md`, `docs/analysis/2026-10-02-drift-rootcause.md`, `-fix-ranking.md`)
- **Cause A, the inner loops are capped.** The gyro I-term (UiMax 20, EMin 2 deg/s) and angle I-term cannot hold the
  steady roll torque. The P-term carries it, leaving a standing attitude error, and the outer loops read that as a push.
- **Cause B, the natural lean.** At hover the drone leans about roll -1.24 / pitch -0.90 deg. With velocity Ki 0 and
  position Ui capped at 2 cm/s, that push needs a standing position error. The push grows as the battery sags.
- A and B partly cancel on roll, so fixing only one makes hover worse (sim). Hence the combined set.
- Sim (WP-10, quoted from `fix-ranking.md`, not re-run tonight): PID S1 hover steady 4.7 -> 1.2 cm; S2 load step
  36.3 -> 2.2 cm; S3 waypoints rms 30.9 -> 4.3 cm. The trajectory FF (F6a) carries the S3 gain.
