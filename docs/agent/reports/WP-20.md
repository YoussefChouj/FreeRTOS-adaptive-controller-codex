- **A, scale.** `OF_VEL_SCALE 1.25f` is applied in one helper, `Of_VelCms(raw, bias) = (raw - bias) * 1.25` (cm/s). It is used by:
  - OF position integration (handheld and flight);
  - the velocity-loop FB;
  - the OF EKF velocity input;
  - the EKF bias rebase (`x[2]`, `x[5]`).
- **B, vertical gate.** `u8 g_of_vgate`, where 1 = gated (initial value 1).
  - While gated:
    - OF position does not integrate;
    - `Of_VgateHold` zeroes U on loc/locs and keeps the integrators frozen, not reset;
    - Des is not re-synced.
  - The gate releases after 60 clear ticks (0.3 s).
- **C, landing pause.** An automatic landing ramp exists, so the pause was implemented.
  - The ramp holds at 0.40 m until |loc err| < 3 cm and |locs FB| < 5 cm/s per axis. Minimum 1 s, timeout 3 s.
  - It then descends; below 0.40 m the gate is active.
  - `LAND_MAX_TICKS` was raised by the pause timeout.
- **D, preset.** `g_of_vgate` and `Ctrler.Z_ratePID.FB` were added to slot1 of `position_hold_of.json`.

## Risks

- **Velocity-loop gain +25%.** The locs FB is scaled, so the loop sees 25% more velocity for the same motion.
  Watch for faster oscillation or overshoot in the first hover. If it appears, lower the locs P by about 20%.
- **No correction below 0.40 m.** Takeoff and the last 0.40 m of landing fly without an OF position fix: zero tilt
  plus trim. Any trim error drifts freely there; the landing pause bounds this to the final 0.40 m.
- **Slow-descent leak.** A vertical speed under 0.2 m/s above 0.40 m is still integrated. The handheld replay shows
  about 2 cm per 0.3 m lowered.
- **`g_ekf_gate` (Ekf9, `TASK/send_data.c` `OF_LSB_MPS 0.01`) is unscaled.** When the EKF velocity path

  the diff to StabilizerTask.c).

  applied to the ungated rows. It is not ground truth, and the new pause changes the real descent.
  The real check is a flight landing measured with tape.

## Hover flight 4 (`logs/vofa/flight_test_hover_4`) and REVERT (2026-10-02 evening)

Operator: lift-off drift to +x, slow wandering around the room (new), worse drift while descending, so they cut the motors.
Measured (slot1, 50 Hz): armed 27.4 s, airborne 21.9 s. The locs U was **exactly 0 for 29% of the airborne time**:

| t (s) | phase | share of time U = 0 |
|---|---|---|
| 16-20 | takeoff | 100% |
| 24 | climb | 43% |
| 36-38 | landing, z 0.79 -> 0.33 m | 79-100% |

The gate did what it was built to do: it zeroed the position and velocity output. That is the fault, because it left
the drone open-loop exactly when wind, prop wash and ground effect push it. The position FB stayed within ±9 cm,
so the estimate never saw the drift. Gating the estimate is defensible. Gating the control output is not.

Scale does not fix landing drift. The hold equilibrium is FB = Des at any scale factor, so x1.25 only raised the
loop gain (velocity loop x1.24 measured: slope of locys FB vs -of2_dx_fix is 1.236 in hover_4 and 1.000 in hover_2).

**Decision: revert the whole WP-20 firmware** (`git checkout ae5d48d -- TASK/StabilizerTask.c presets/position_hold_of.json`).
- The restored file is the firmware hover_2 flew: it includes bb2040c (debiased velocity FB), and hover_2's slope is 1.000.
- Build (copied into `../wt-night`, `UV4 -b`): 0 errors, the same 5 warnings, Code 108620.
- `ground_station/research/of_gate_replay.py` and its tests stay as research tools; their constants describe the
  reverted gate, not the flashed firmware.

## The real landing-drift cause (next WP, not done)

`flight_phase` becomes FLYING only at `Z_posPID.FB > 0.2 m` (StabilizerTask.c, takeoff transition), and OF position
integrates only while FLYING or LANDING. So the first 0.2 m of lift-off is never measured or corrected, and the hold
reference is set wherever the drone happens to be at 0.2 m. Proposed fix:
1. integrate OF position from arm/spool-up (quality OK), so the home point is the takeoff spot;
2. keep the controllers always active; never zero U;
3. make the landing descend toward the home point;
4. acceptance: a tape-measured landing offset vs the takeoff spot.
