- **A, scale.** `OF_VEL_SCALE 1.25f` is applied in one helper, `Of_VelCms(raw, bias) = (raw - bias) * 1.25` (cm/s). It is used by:
  - OF position integration (handheld and flight);
  - the velocity-loop FB;
  - the OF EKF velocity input;
  - the EKF bias rebase (`x[2]`, `x[5]`).
- **B, vertical gate.** `u8 g_of_vgate`, where 1 = gated (initial value 1).
  - While gated:
    - OF position does not integrate;
    - `Of_VgateHold` zeroes U on loc/locs and keeps the integrators frozen, not reset;
    - Des is not re-synced.
  - The gate releases after 60 clear ticks (0.3 s).
- **C, landing pause.** An automatic landing ramp exists, so the pause was implemented.
  - The ramp holds at 0.40 m until |loc err| < 3 cm and |locs FB| < 5 cm/s per axis. Minimum 1 s, timeout 3 s.
  - It then descends; below 0.40 m the gate is active.
  - `LAND_MAX_TICKS` was raised by the pause timeout.
- **D, preset.** `g_of_vgate` and `Ctrler.Z_ratePID.FB` were added to slot1 of `position_hold_of.json`.

## Risks

- **Velocity-loop gain +25%.** The locs FB is scaled, so the loop sees 25% more velocity for the same motion.
  Watch for faster oscillation or overshoot in the first hover. If it appears, lower the locs P by about 20%.
- **No correction below 0.40 m.** Takeoff and the last 0.40 m of landing fly without an OF position fix: zero tilt
  plus trim. Any trim error drifts freely there; the landing pause bounds this to the final 0.40 m.
- **Slow-descent leak.** A vertical speed under 0.2 m/s above 0.40 m is still integrated. The handheld replay shows
  about 2 cm per 0.3 m lowered.
- **`g_ekf_gate` (Ekf9, `TASK/send_data.c` `OF_LSB_MPS 0.01`) is unscaled.** When the EKF velocity path
  (`g_ekf_gate.vx_cms`) is selected as the locs FB, it uses x1.00 OF. That path is off by default: `ctrl_enable_req` is
  zero-initialised and no firmware code sets it, only a dashboard write. It was left out of scope (the brief limits
  the diff to StabilizerTask.c).
- **The landing info line grew** (+5.5 -> +8.2 cm in x). It is OF's own reading on an old-firmware flight, with x1.25
  applied to the ungated rows. It is not ground truth, and the new pause changes the real descent.
  The real check is a flight landing measured with tape.
