## Risks

- Mass unverified → k_T accuracy uncertain (scales linearly).
- Per-motor k_T unknown (CW/CCW props may differ).
- Corner mapping unresolved → roll/pitch torque estimates are CW/CCW differential, not per-corner.
- Firmware change: `imu_data.a_acc[_Z]` → `Lin_Acc_Z_body` (mg→m/s² conversion). `Lin_Acc_Z_body` is gravity-removed body-frame Z accel, in mg, computed by Mahony each update.
- RPM median-of-5 replaces mean-of-4. With one missed or extra edge, the mean is off by a quarter of the bad period's error.
  The median is still one of the four good periods.
- `g_thrust_est.empirical[]` no longer constant 12.8-12.9 (bug fixed: LUT now in ticks, not µs).
