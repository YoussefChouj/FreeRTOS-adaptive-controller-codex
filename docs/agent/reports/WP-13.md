## Budget
Slot 1 adds 25 B, so a frame goes from 124 to 149 B: about 2.5 to 3.0 kB/s at a nominal 20 Hz (16 Hz measured).
It runs on the 91.3 kB/s USART3 WiFi wire, so there is plenty of room.

## Risks
- The trim assumes the lean belongs to the body. If the rotated-takeoff flight shows it belongs to the room, zero `g_att_trim_*`.
- The gate holds the integrators at 0 until FLYING, so takeoff starts without the I-term. The trim covers most of the lean.
- FF uses a finite difference over an assumed 10 ms. Tick jitter makes `locxsPID.Des` spike, and Kd acts on error.
  Watch locxsPID.Des on the waypoint flight.

## Flight acceptance (operator)
1. Hover 60 s, PID only: steady error under 2 cm per axis, no 0.9 Hz sway.
2. A 1 m step: it settles with no sustained oscillation. Record the overshoot and the settling time.
3. Asymmetric-load hover, PID then MRAC: compare the steady error with the sim's S2 (2.2 cm steady, 5.0 rms).
4. Dense waypoints (1 m square at 0.2 m/s): compare the rms with the sim's S3 (4.3 cm; F0 was 30.9).
