## Firmware changes between the two days (`git diff 236ba13^1 236ba13 -- TASK/StabilizerTask.c API/ekf_of.c`)

- Attitude trim from the WP-9 hover means: `g_att_trim_roll_deg = -1.24f`, `g_att_trim_pitch_deg = -0.90f`.
- `ComputePID_Gated(..., airborne)` on the loc, locs, angle and gyro PIDs: the PIDs are gated while the drone is not
  airborne. Read `ComputePID_Gated` to see exactly what it freezes.
- OF EKF went from 6 to 8 states (accel bias). It uses accel input with `EKF_OF_ACC_SIGN_X +1`,
  `EKF_OF_ACC_SIGN_Y -1`, a zero-velocity update on the ground, and one update per new OF frame.
- Trajectory feed-forward is active only during WFB trajectory execution, so it should be inert in hover. Confirm this.

## Hypotheses: rank them with numbers and kill the ones the data rejects

H1. The trim over-corrects or has the wrong sign. The WP-9 lean was partly carried by the I-terms already, or the
    trim's sign/frame is wrong, so the steady tilt now leans the other way. Test: steady roll/pitch Des-FB and angle
    I-terms (slot1/slot2) yesterday vs today. Compare the drift acceleration the trim implies (g*tan(trim)) with the
    observed drift and with the lean the velocity loop demands (locxsPID.U / locysPID.U).
H2. OF bias or EKF: a non-zero `s_of_bias_*` shifts what the hold loop treats as "stationary", so the real position
    walks. Test: which position does the hold loop use (bias-corrected or raw)? Compare the drift rate with the
    bias, `earth_x` vs `earth_x_ture`, and the `s_ekf_of.x` states. Also check `g_of_bias_mode`.
H3. Setup or environment: takeoff heading, floor covering, battery, CoG. Read both meta.json files and the f17 metas.
H4. Ground gating changed the takeoff transient. Check whether the drift starts at takeoff or builds during the hold.
