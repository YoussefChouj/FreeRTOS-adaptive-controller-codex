## Item 1 / 12 finding (2026-10-12 22:50, measured)

The live tracker's fixes **stick to static spots**. This is a drift mechanism for modes 2/3: a stuck fix feeds the
position loop "you are still" while the drone moves.

- **r6_f4_sysid (mode 1, so the EKF is unfused).** Over 255-375 s the fixes sit at (-0.83, -1.24) m. In the same
  15 s windows:

  | Source | Spread (cm) | Median position (m) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Live fixes | 2-7 | (-0.83, -1.24), fixed |
  | Offline coral truth | 54-89 | moves over -0.7..0.1, -0.3..0.5 |
  | Earth estimate | 59-96 | — |

  The fit scale of 0.27 and the 118 cm rms from item 1 come from this lock, not from the floor pose.
- **truth4 sync works with `--fixes`.** The fixes take-off gives an offset of 159.79 s and the telemetry gives
  159.73 s. truth4 (video truth) stands.
- **Stuck 5 s windows** (fix spread < 2 cm, more than 0.3 m from the pad):

  | Flight | Stuck windows | Where (m) |
  |---|---|---|
  | f2 | 6 of 25 | (0.26, 0.28) |
  | f3 | 11 of 22 | (-2.17, -8.08) |
  | f3b | 4 of 38 | (-0.74, -1.12), same spot as f4 |
  | f4 | 9 of 59 | (-0.83, -1.24) |
  | f4b | 2 of 60 | (-1.19, 10.33) |
  | f4c | 5 of 53 | (-0.03, -0.33) |

  Only f4 has been checked against truth. The other flights are candidates; a hover can also be still.
- **Root cause.** Live, the coral tracker had no scenery mask (`None if src_live`), while the offline truth masks
  coral scenery over the whole video (`vt.coral_static`). A coral-coloured object near the path stayed inside the ROI
  with >= min_px pixels, so the fix never missed and never re-seeded.
- **Fix (tracker only, no flash).** `CoralTracker` learns the mask from the start: coral in >= 70 % of 30 frames
  (every 5th) with the drone on the pad, minus a 1.3 roi disc around the seed (the drone's coral reaches ~170 px
  of 1920 on the r6_f4 pad frame, measured). New flag `--coral-static auto|video|start|none`; auto = start live.
