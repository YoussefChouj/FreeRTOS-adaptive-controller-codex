## Camera pose check (operator tape measure vs floor-1018 scan)

| | Tape (drone frame) | floor-1018 scan (pad-marker frame, cam_r4_lenswave) |
|---|---|---|
| x | +1.50..1.53 m | -1.643 m |
| y | +2.00..2.05 m | -2.218 m |
| height | +1.90..1.93 m | +1.995 m |
| distance along the floor | 2.53 m | 2.76 m (+9 %) |
| bearing | 53.2 deg | -126.5 deg (180.3 deg apart) |

The bearings differ by 180 deg within 0.3 deg: the pad marker's x/y axes point opposite the drone's axes. That is
UNVERIFIED as the intended convention: it can also be a 180 deg error that the tracker's live fit absorbs. The scan
puts the camera 9 % farther along the floor and 4 % higher than the tape. Possible causes, not yet checked: the tape
ran to the camera body, not the lens centre; the pad centre is not the marker centre; the 0.199 m marker side or the
focal length is off. Effect near the pad is a few cm. Before relying on this geometry for absolute positions, check it
against a known floor point.
