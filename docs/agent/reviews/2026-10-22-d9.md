## Bottom line

No crash or wrong-gain bug found. Two findings matter for the thesis: with ADRC / LQR / MPC selected, MRAC wraps
a **different nominal controller** than the one its reference model and comment assume (b), and both layers then
cancel the same disturbance (c). That is item D3. The adrc-plus-mrac abort is a host timing issue: a write the
firmware applied is reported as failed when the host's ingest lag is longer than the 1 s timeout (f).
