# A6 - filter choice from the A5 simulation

For the linear Z model with attitude supplied, a steady-state three-state Kalman filter (h, vz, acceleration bias) has the same predict-plus-innovation structure as the CF; its distinction here is the Riccati-selected fixed gain, so an EKF adds no model benefit within this scope. In [A5](z-cf-rootcause-A5.md), at the PROPOSED 50 ms range delay / 40 ms motor lag and runtime position Ki=0.001, the delayed KF reduced true-height hover RMS from 1.26 cm (CF+A2+A3) to 0.42 cm and step overshoot from 3.68 cm to 2.78 cm, while the approximate phase margins were essentially equal (66.36 versus 66.53 deg); the KF did not meet the strict 4 mm settle band within 25 s, whereas the CF settled at 24.09 s. This RMS improvement justifies a KF candidate for an offline replay and subsequent operator-approved bench comparison after latency/noise identification, **not a blanket recommendation to replace the flown CF**: the plant and Q/R are PROPOSED, the startup bias transient contributes to RMS, and the study failed to reproduce the reported CF wobble (largest simulated CF half peak-to-peak 2.59 cm versus reported 10-15 cm). Keep timing-correct fixed CF as the baseline; choose KF gains only if the improvement survives aligned measured data and comparable tuning, without losing margin or settling performance.
