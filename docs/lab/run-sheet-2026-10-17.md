## Branches & fallbacks

| Event | Do |
|---|---|
| Flash exits non-zero | Stop. Read the output; don't retry with `--force` |
| F1 heading swings or drifts | Land. No more flights on this image; keep the log for the yaw fix (37ecf87e) |
| E1-E4 refuse at the exp read-back | `exp_active` not streamed: firmware not flashed or 8081 not restarted |
| Ladder rung refused `0xEE` | speed above the lim row's trajv: don't edit move_s at the lab, skip that rung |
| Tilt trip or fence stop on a rung | That rung is the limit for today: log it, don't fly the next rung |
| F1 takeoff drift | `.\fly lab_c_robust --gains c2 --once` or `--post-takeoff-gains c3` (10-16 sheet) |
| "NO FIX SENT" during a flight | land, check the tracker, re-fly |
