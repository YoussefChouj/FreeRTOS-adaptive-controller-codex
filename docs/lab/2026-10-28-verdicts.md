Bottom line: the 2.3-2.9 Hz wobble is the rate loop's own crossover with too little phase margin (PM 40-45 deg).
Every flight shows it, baseline PID included; live MRAC injection doubles its size. Fix the rate loop first
(proposal below), then refly the thesis MRAC flights on the new rate gains.

## LQR: why the first version worked and the later ones did not

The cause is the Smith-predictor delay (VAR 47 `g_xyc_delay_ticks`). The LQR gains were designed for 33 ticks.

| LQR flight | Predictor delay | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 10-15 screen | 33 (compiled, before VAR 47 existed) | complete, hover rms 4/3 cm, max 11/10 |
| 10-21 exp lqr-3l | 12 | oscillation abort |
| 10-21 lab-c lqr | 12 | hover max 42/24 cm, firmware landing |
| 10-24 exp lqr-3l-u0 | 21 | hover rms 11/11, traj rms 22/19 cm |
| 10-28 diag + fix ladder | 12, 18 + lead | operator aborts after 3.5-33 s |

- On 10-15 VAR 47 was added with a firmware default of 12, chosen from `xy_delay_sim.py` and never measured. The firmware
  applies it on the ground at boot, so every LQR/MPC flight after that ran a shorter predictor than the gains assume.
- The camera lag measured on 10-21 is 330 ms, which is 33 ticks.
- The "2 Hz limit cycle" blamed on 33 is the 2.3-2.9 Hz rate-loop wobble that every flight shows (section above).
  It belongs to the rate loop, not to the predictor.
- Fix: VAR 47 is back to 33 in every LQR/MPC card, and the firmware default is now 33 (needs the flash). The
  lab-e-model cards drop the predictor lead. The `_d12` and diag cards keep 12 on purpose.
- Remaining high-frequency wobble: fix it through the rate-loop step above, not through the predictor.
