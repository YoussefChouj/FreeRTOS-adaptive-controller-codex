# 2026-10-27/28 flights: verdicts

Bottom line: the 2.3-2.9 Hz wobble is the rate loop's own crossover with too little phase margin (PM 40-45 deg).
Every flight shows it, baseline PID included; live MRAC injection doubles its size. Fix the rate loop first
(proposal below), then refly the thesis MRAC flights on the new rate gains.

## Wobble (thesis MRAC flights + baseline)

| Flight | Gyro std in the air (deg/s) | Peak |
|---|---|---|
| baseline PID, angle-shadow | 4.3-4.6 | 2.3-2.9 Hz |
| angle-live | 8.8 | 2.3-2.9 Hz |
| vel-live | 8.2 | 2.3-2.9 Hz |

- The peak sits at the rate-loop crossover (wc 19 rad/s = 3 Hz, from the sysid chain below).
- At the wobble frequency u_ad is about 2% of u_nom (25 Hz log). The direct injection is too small to cause it; MRAC
  pushes a loop that is already close to its limit. The 25 Hz log cannot see content above 12.5 Hz.
- Open: the xy-inner live flight also wobbled. Check that the attitude u_ad is reset between flights.
