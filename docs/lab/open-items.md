**Tests (operator, 2026-10-23, standing).** Tests exist for when agents are building or changing something. When a
test is found broken, ineffective or buggy, and it does not block the flights in progress, it is filed here and
worked on after the lab session ends, or when the operator instructs an agent to go over them, whichever comes
first. Same-day at the bench is not required, and mid-lab work on tests is not offered. The reason: fixing and
running tests does not affect the experiments being flown.
