# Run sheet 2026-10-10: names to paste, Keil values per flight

Full guide: `docs/lab/today-2026-10-10.md`. This page is the short version for the bench.

## Every boot (each new pack), before the first takeoff: only check, nothing to write

| check | value |
|---|---|
| pack voltage | 16.8 V (gate lands at 14.3 V under load) |
| `kp_active` | 7 |
| `g_ctrl_axis_mask` | 63 (all 6 axes, x/y MRAC on by default now) |
| `g_land_gate_enable` | 3 |
| `lab_stage_active` | 1. A new pack reboots to stage 1: if you were on another stage, write `lab_stage` again |

## Per flight: three things in Keil

| step | PID | MRAC row NN |
|---|---|---|
| 1, on the ground | `lab_stage = S` (stage table below), wait for `lab_stage_active = S` | same |
| 2 | ch8 off (`vp_id` does not matter) | `vp_id = NN`, ch8 on |
| 3 | take off, hover, `traj_go = 1` | same |

`lab_stage` sets the path and the limits in one write (figure-8, `traj_id 4`). It applies only on the ground,
so change it before takeoff. 0xEE in `lab_stage_active` = bad stage number.

| `lab_stage` | tilt | laps | yaw rate | `lim_mode` | flights |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 (flat) | 1 | 90 | 0 | nose check, x/y shakedown |
| 1 (boot) | 30 | 2 | 90 | 0 | no load |
| 2 | 30 | 3 | 120 | 1 | no load |
| 3 | 30 | 4 | 160 | 2 | no load |
| 4 | 30 | 2 | 90 | 0 | 0.2 kg under the front-left motor |
| 5 | 30 | 2 | 90 | 0 | 0.2 kg front-left + 0.4 kg centre |

Fixed defaults you never touch: path size 70 cm, `move_s` 20, `yaw_follow` 1, `start_fixed` 1 (start at 0, 0,
0.85 m). PID flights with ch8 off: every MRAC axis, x/y included, adds zero (one gate in `controller.c`).

## Watch during the flight

| variable | normal | act |
|---|---|---|
| `g_ctrl_axis_mask` | 63 | 15: `kp_id` was changed off 7, write `kp_id = 7` |
| `traj_phase` | 4 (goto), 5 (settle), 1 (move), then return | stuck: `traj_stop = 1` |
| `lab_stage_active`, `lim_active` | stage you wrote, its `lim_mode` | not equal: land, rewrite `lab_stage` |
| `g_land_gate_trip` | 0 | 1 = low voltage landed it: swap pack |
| `g_land_gate_sat_n`, `g_land_gate_sat_max_s` | 0 | write both down after landing |
| load swing | settles | grows over 3 swings: ch8 off (PID), land |

## Drone geometry (operator measured 2026-10-10)

| item | value |
|---|---|
| white guard | front-left motor, at +x, -y from the centre |
| guard diameter | 29 cm, centred on each motor |
| motor centre to motor centre | 27 cm adjacent, 38 cm diagonal |
| overall span | 57 cm side to side, 68 cm corner to corner |

Computed from these: arm 19 cm, motors at x, y = ±13.5 cm. 27 + 29 = 56 and 38 + 29 = 67 match the measured 57 and
68 within 1 cm. A 0.2 kg load under one motor gives 0.2 × 9.81 × 0.135 = 0.26 N·m on both pitch and roll.
Path at tilt 30 is x ±35, y ±61, z 0.50 to 1.20 m; with the 34 cm guard radius the drone sweeps about 1.4 × 1.9 m.
Lowest path point is 0.50 m, so keep sling plus load under about 0.4 m or the load reaches the floor (computed).

where the load hooks on (under the motor centre or at the guard rim); sling length per flight; flight-area size.
