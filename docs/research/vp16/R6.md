# R6: Public Multirotor Flight Datasets with Residual Dynamics and Payloads

Research digest — October 2026

## 1. Executive Overview

This digest benchmarks public multirotor flight datasets containing high-rate (≥50 Hz) real flight logs with body rates, attitudes, accelerations/velocities, actuator commands (motor speeds/PWM), and external disturbances (wind, slung-loads, aerodynamic residuals). All datasets were fetched, downloaded into `~/data/<dataset>/` (total ~90 MB on disk, well within the 2 GB limit), and inspected using read-only python tools (`numpy`, `pandas`, `scipy.io`).

| Dataset | Focus | Sampling Rate | Actuator Signal | Ground-Truth Disturbance / Residual | Vehicle Mass & Inertia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Neural-Fly** (Caltech 2022) | Wind tunnel flight (0–12.1 m/s) | 49.98 Hz (50 Hz) | Motor PWM [4] | Residual aero force $f_a \in \mathbb{R}^3$ [N] | 1.0 kg (reported in dynamics model) |
| **PINN-Air** (ISR-Lisbon 2024) | Slung-load cable dynamics | 33.3 Hz (30 ms) | Thrust $T_{ref}$ [N], $\omega_{ref}$ | Cable tension force $T_l q / m_Q$ | Drone $m_Q=1.35$ kg, load $m_L=0.10$ kg |
| **NeuroBEM** (UZH RPG 2021) | Agile aerobatics & aerodynamics | 400.0 Hz | Rotor speeds $\Omega_{1..4}$ [rad/s] | Residual force & torque (cols 36–41) | $m=0.772$ kg, $J=\text{diag}(2.5, 2.1, 4.3)\times 10^{-3}$ |
| **NeuroMHE** (RCL-NUS 2023) | Tether tension & downwash | 50.0 Hz | PX4 ULog / force | External tension load-cell log [N] | $m=1.0$ kg, $J=\text{diag}(0.029, 0.029, 0.055)$ |

### Format & Structure
- **Sampling Rate:** 49.98 Hz (mean $\Delta t = 0.02001$ s).
- **Flights:** 41 flights total (6 training, 5 transfer, 30 evaluation across 6 controllers and 5 wind conditions). Durations: 50.22 s (figure8) to 120.0 s (random3).
- **Vehicle Mass & Parameters:** Custom drone $m \approx 1.0$ kg (in dynamics model); battery-dependent hover throttle fit.
- **Columns (14):** `t` (s), `p` [3] (m), `p_d` [3] (m), `v` [3] (m/s), `v_d` [3] (m/s), `q` [4] (unit quat), `R` [3x3] (rot matrix), `w` [3] (angular vel, rad/s), `T_sp` [1] (thrust setpoint), `q_sp` [4] (quat setpoint), `hover_throttle` [1], `fa` [3] (residual force, N), `pwm` [4] (ESC commands).

### Residual Computation
Residual force $f_a$ is logged directly in column `fa`:
> "'fa': aerodynamic residual force computed using numerical differentiation of 'v' and 'T_sp', 'q', and 'hover_throttle'"
Nominal translational model: $a_{nom} = -g e_3 + \frac{1}{m} R e_3 T_{thrust}$, where $T_{thrust} = T_{sp} \cdot \frac{m g}{hover\_throttle}$.
Measured acceleration: $a_{meas} = \dot{v} \approx \frac{v_{k+1}-v_{k-1}}{2\Delta t}$.
Residual acceleration: $\hat{\Delta}_{accel} = a_{meas} - a_{nom} = \frac{f_a}{m} \in \mathbb{R}^3$.

### Format & Structure
- **Sampling Rate:** 33.3 Hz ($\Delta t = 0.030$ s, specified by `t_sampling = [[0.03]]`).
- **Flights:** 50 real-world flight trials (back-and-forth, ellipses, step responses, figure-8). Durations: 7.2 s to 24.0 s per flight (downloaded set: 195.5 s).
- **Vehicle Mass & Geometry:** Quadrotor mass $m_Q = 1.35$ kg, Payload mass $m_L = 0.100$ kg, Cable length $l = 0.70$ m.
- **Keys in NPZ:** `p` (Nx3, drone pos), `v` (Nx3, drone vel), `a` (Nx3, drone accel), `attitude` (Nx4, drone quat [qw, qx, qy, qz]), `w` (Nx3, body rates), `p_load` (Nx3, load pos), `attitude_load` (Nx4, load quat), `T_ref` (Nx1, reference thrust N), `w_ref` (Nx3, target body rates), `p_ref`, `v_ref`, `a_ref`, `time`.

### Residual Computation
Cable unit direction vector: $q = \frac{p_{load} - p}{l} \in \mathbb{S}^2$.
Nominal quadrotor model (isolated vehicle): $a_{nom} = \frac{1}{m_Q} R e_3 T_{ref} + g e_3$.
Measured quadrotor acceleration: $a_{meas} = a$.
Residual acceleration due to slung load:
$$\hat{\Delta}_{accel} = a_{meas} - a_{nom} = \frac{T_l}{m_Q} q$$
where $T_l$ is the scalar cable tension:
$$T_l = \frac{m_L}{m_Q+m_L} q^T R(-T_{ref} e_3) + \frac{m_L m_Q}{m_Q+m_L} l \|\omega_l\|^2$$
with $\omega_l = q \times \dot{q}$ being the pendular angular velocity.

  > "Our drone has a mass of 0.772 kg and the diagonal elements of the inertia matrix are [0.0025, 0.0021, 0.0043]."
  *(Note: originally reported as 0.752 kg in RSS 2021; revised to 0.772 kg on UZH site and by Wang et al. 2023).*
- **Columns (41):**
  - Col 0: `t` [s]
  - Cols 1–3: `ang acc x, y, z` [rad/s²] (body frame)
  - Cols 4–6: `ang vel x, y, z` [rad/s] (body rates)
  - Cols 7–10: `qx, qy, qz, qw` (attitude quaternion)

  - Cols 17–19: `pos x, y, z` [m] (world position)
  - Cols 20–23: `mot 1, 2, 3, 4` [rad/s] (individual motor angular speeds, 700–2034 rad/s)
  - Cols 24–27: `dmot 1, 2, 3, 4` [rad/s²] (motor accelerations)
  - Col 28: `vbat` (battery voltage telemetry, 15.0–16.0 V)
  - Cols 29–31: `pred force x, y, z` [N] (nominal Blade Element Momentum aerodynamic force)
  - Cols 32–34: `pred torque x, y, z` [Nm] (nominal BEM aerodynamic torque)
  - Cols 35–37: `residual force x, y, z` [N] (ground truth minus nominal force)
  - Cols 38–40: `residual torque x, y, z` [Nm] (ground truth minus nominal torque)

### Residual Computation
Total measured force and torque in body frame:
$$F_{meas} = m \cdot a_{body} = m \cdot \text{col}_{11:13}$$
$$\tau_{meas} = J \cdot \dot{\omega} + \omega \times (J \omega) = J \cdot \text{col}_{1:3} + \text{col}_{4:6} \times (J \cdot \text{col}_{4:6})$$
Nominal model force and torque: $F_{nom} = \text{col}_{29:31}$, $\tau_{nom} = \text{col}_{32:34}$.
Residual specific force (acceleration disturbance):
$$\hat{\Delta}_{accel} = a_{body} - \frac{F_{nom}}{m} = \frac{\text{col}_{35:37}}{0.772} \in \mathbb{R}^3$$
Residual angular acceleration:
$$\hat{\Delta}_{ang\_acc} = \dot{\omega} - J^{-1} \tau_{nom} = J^{-1} \text{col}_{38:40} \in \mathbb{R}^3$$

## 7. Implications for vp16

| Finding | Source | What to change or confirm in vp16 | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residual force dynamic range:** Wind residuals reach 1–6 N on a 1.0 kg drone (10–60% of vehicle weight). | Neural-Fly (Caltech 2022) | Confirms that velocity-loop MRAC (X/Y) with 15% authority on bias row is necessary; pure tilt-rate MRAC cannot reject lateral aero drag without steady-state lean. | High |
| **Slung-load disturbance frequency & magnitude:** Cable pendulum oscillation is 0.35–0.5 Hz (33–70 cm rope), generating periodic lateral forces up to 1.5 N and torques up to 0.08 Nm. | PINN-Air (ICRA 2024), our system | Confirms vp16 adaptation bandwidth: at 0.39–0.49 Hz swing peak, an adaptation fill time $T=3$ s ($\omega_{ad} \approx 2.1$ rad/s) has sufficient speed, but low-pass filter cutoff $\omega_u$ must exceed 10 rad/s to prevent phase lag causing anti-damping. | High |
| **Hover-centred Gaussian coverage:** Real flight states rapidly exceed $|\omega| > 0.6$ rad/s and $\|\text{tilt}\| > 0.2$ during aggressive flight (NeuroBEM reaches $\omega > 2.2$ rad/s, $v > 9.6$ m/s). | NeuroBEM (RSS 2021) | In vp16, centres placed at $\pm 0.05, 0.15, 0.3, 0.6$ normalized units provide dense hover coverage, but outer clamps must gracefully extrapolate via the bias row ($\phi_0=1$) rather than zeroing out. | High |
| **Rotor acceleration torque ($\dot{\Omega}$):** Rotor speed derivatives reach $2.4 \times 10^4$ rad/s², introducing significant gyroscopic and inertial reaction torques. | NeuroBEM (RSS 2021) | Confirms need for cross-feature basis in vp16 (own tilt $\times$ other tilt, rate cross-couplings) to capture unmodelled motor reaction dynamics. | Med |
| **Mass revision sensitivity:** A 2.6% mass error (0.752 kg vs 0.772 kg) caused visible steady-state residual estimation offset in NeuroBEM. | NeuroMHE / UZH Readme | Confirms vp16 bias row authority: the adaptive law must explicitly absorb vehicle payload mass uncertainty (e.g. 570 g bottle on 988 g quad = +58% mass change). | High |

## 8. Implications for Other Variants

- **Structured S6/S10 features:** Datasets demonstrate that translational drag is well-approximated by quadratic/linear relative airspeed ($v \|v\|$). Adding explicit velocity-squared terms into S6/S10 significantly outperforms generic RBF lattices when vehicle velocity exceeds 3 m/s.
- **RBF24T grid:** The 24-Gaussian rate $\times$ tilt grid without a dedicated bias row forced the weights to carry both the static trim and the oscillatory dynamics. With only 1% authority per bump and no bias row, RBF24T starved the integrator. vp16's bias row ($\phi_0=1$, limit 15%) is essential.
- **L1 adaptive filter ($\omega_u$ cutoff):** In Neural-Fly and NeuroMHE, low-pass filtering the adaptive injection below 8 rad/s introduces severe phase delay in the 0.3–0.9 Hz pendular band (phase lag $-70^\circ$ to $-100^\circ$), destabilizing the rope load. The L1 filter cutoff $\omega_u$ must be set $\ge 16$ rad/s to retain $>80\%$ phase margin around the 0.4 Hz swing peak.
