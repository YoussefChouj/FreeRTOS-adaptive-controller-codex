- **Problem Addressed:** Robustness issues due to concentrated adaptation gain, and tracking errors. Set-theoretic MRAC explicitly restricts the tracking error to a user-defined set.
- **Implementation Complexity:** M
- **Bench Candidate:** Set-Theoretic MRAC
  - **Law:** $\dot{\hat{\theta}} = \Gamma x e^T P$ with a generalized restricted potential function barrier $V_e(e)$ that drives adaptation gain up as $e$ approaches the boundary.
  - **Knobs:** Base learning rate $\Gamma$, boundary limit parameter $e_{max}$.
  - **Expected effect:** Prevents reference-model mismatch from exceeding predefined bounds, boosting robustness.
  - **Risk:** High control effort near the boundary causing actuator saturation.

## 2. Closed-Loop Reference Models (CRM)
- **Works Cited:**
  - *Adaptive Systems with Closed-loop Reference Models: Stability, Robustness and Transient Performance* (2012, Gibson, Annaswamy, Lavretsky), arXiv:1201.4897. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.4897. Quote: "This paper explores the properties of adaptive systems with closed-loop reference models."
- **Problem Addressed:** Reference-model mismatch of 18-27% and high-frequency oscillations. CRM adds feedback to the reference model itself so it tracks the plant during transients.
- **Implementation Complexity:** S
- **Bench Candidate:** CRM Adaptive Controller
  - **Law:** $\dot{x}_m = A_m x_m + B_m r + L(x - x_m)$, with adaptation $\dot{\hat{\theta}} = \Gamma x (x - x_m)^T P$.
  - **Knobs:** Observer gain matrix $L$, adaptation gain $\Gamma$.
  - **Expected effect:** Dramatically lowers reference-model mismatch by slowing the reference model to accommodate plant limits.
  - **Risk:** Reduces tracking speed on aggressive step inputs.

## 3. Composite / Concurrent-Learning MRAC
- **Works Cited:**
  - *Deep Model Reference Adaptive Control* (2019, Joshi & Chowdhary), arXiv:1909.08602. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08602. Quote: "We present a new neuroadaptive architecture: Deep Neural Network based Model Reference Adaptive Control"
- **Problem Addressed:** Lack of parameter convergence and robustness on steps. Concurrent learning buffers past data to satisfy rank conditions without persistent excitation.
- **Implementation Complexity:** M
- **Bench Candidate:** Concurrent Learning MRAC (CL-MRAC)
  - **Law:** $\dot{\hat{W}} = \Gamma (\Phi(x) e^T P + \sum_{i=1}^N \Phi(x_i) \epsilon_i^T)$, where $\epsilon_i$ is the historic modeling error.
  - **Knobs:** History buffer size $N$, singular value threshold for recording data, learning rate $\Gamma$.
  - **Expected effect:** Enhances long-term parameter convergence and decoupling of adaptive gains.
  - **Risk:** Buffer management overhead and potentially stale data points affecting rapid transitions.

## 4. Adaptive Control Barrier Functions (aCBF)
- **Works Cited:**
  - *Adaptive Safety with Control Barrier Functions* (2019, Taylor & Ames), arXiv:1910.00555. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.00555. Quote: "Our proposed approach adaptively achieves safety by keeping the systems state within a safe set"
- **Problem Addressed:** Actuator saturation and catastrophic failure states. aCBF acts as a safety filter over any controller.
- **Implementation Complexity:** L
- **Bench Candidate:** aCBF-QP Filter
  - **Law:** $u = \text{argmin}_u ||u - u_{nom}||^2$ subject to $L_f h + L_g h u + \alpha(h) + \hat{\theta}^T \phi(x) \ge 0$.
  - **Knobs:** Class-K function $\alpha$, parameter estimation bounds.
  - **Expected effect:** Prevents saturation by formally restricting commands that would exceed motor capabilities or flip the drone.
  - **Risk:** QP solver computational cost on a microcontroller.

## 5. SINDy Family
- **Works Cited:**
  - *Discovering governing equations from data: Sparse identification of nonlinear dynamical systems* (2016, Brunton), arXiv:1509.03580. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03580. Quote: "The ability to discover physical laws and governing equations from data is one of humankind's"
- **Problem Addressed:** Identifying physics-based features dominating at varying scales to act as adaptive regressors.
- **Implementation Complexity:** M
- **Bench Candidate:** SINDy-Augmented FF
  - **Law:** STLSQ algorithm offline to find $\dot{x} = \Theta(x)\Xi$. Online FF uses identified sparse terms $u_{FF} = -\Theta(x_{ref})\Xi$.
  - **Knobs:** Sparsity threshold $\lambda$, polynomial library size.
  - **Expected effect:** Accurate prediction of nonlinear drag and cross-coupling without hand-derived models.
  - **Risk:** Susceptibility to measurement noise when calculating state derivatives.

## 6. Neuroadaptive Control
- **Works Cited:**
  - *Neural-Fly Enables Rapid Learning for Agile Flight in Strong Winds* (2022, O'Connell), Science Robotics / arXiv:2205.06908. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06908. Quote: "Executing safe and precise flight maneuvers in dynamic high-speed winds is important for the ongoing"
- **Problem Addressed:** 3-layer MRAC predictive layer does nothing on steps. Deep MRAC / Neural-Fly learns complex representations offline and adapts a linear mix online.
- **Implementation Complexity:** L
- **Bench Candidate:** Neural-Fly Meta-Learning MRAC
  - **Law:** $u = u_{nom} - \hat{W}^T \phi_{NN}(x)$, with $\dot{\hat{W}}$ driven by composite adaptation.
  - **Knobs:** Number of basis functions, meta-learning rate, adaptation gain.
  - **Expected effect:** Substantial improvement on held-out families by generalizing aerodynamics.
  - **Risk:** High latency in NN forward pass during real-time control.

## Pre-registration Suggestions
1. **Set-Theoretic MRAC:** Will strictly cap the maximum transient error during the 'disturbances' family but will hit the saturation limit 50% more often than baseline.
2. **CRM Adaptive:** Will reduce the 18-27% reference model mismatch to under 5%, but at the cost of a 20% slower rise time on the 'steps' family.
3. **CL-MRAC:** Will maintain stable parameter estimates during the 'sines' family without drifting, outperforming baseline MRAC.
4. **aCBF-QP Filter:** Will explicitly eliminate constraint violations in the 'saturation' family but may cause jerky control inputs.
5. **SINDy FF:** Will perfectly model the cross-coupling in the 'chirps' family, reducing overall tracking error by 10%.
6. **Neural-Fly MRAC:** Will outperform the 3-layer MRAC on the 'combo_unseen' and 'motor_loss' families due to better generalized basis functions.
7. **GE Compensator:** Will reduce altitude drop by 80% during the 'ground_effect' held-out test family.
8. **Flatness FF:** Will completely decouple the predictive layer's reliance on feedback, shifting 90% of the control effort to the feedforward term on steps.
