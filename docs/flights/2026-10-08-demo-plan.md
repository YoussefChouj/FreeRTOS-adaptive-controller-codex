## 1. Fair limits (operator ask 1)

MRAC's z output `u_ad` is added after the `Z_ratePID` clamp, so on 10-07 MRAC had thrust headroom PID never had
(flown clamps UMax/UiMax/SumEMax 300/100/250). Presets 5 and 6 widen PID's clamps to 500/300/700 (`zhd`).

| preset | of1 | MRAC axes | Z_ratePID clamps | use today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | off | p, r, y, z | 500/300/700 | main demo (fair) |
| 6 | off | z only | 500/300/700 | fallback if p/r MRAC still swings |
| 1 | off | p, r, y, z | 300/100/250 (flown) | "PID sinks" story; relies on the clamp bypass |
| 0 | on | p, r, y, z | 300/100/250 | = 10-07 flown |

Bench (measured overnight, plant model): PID with zhd holds 1.01 m vs 0.48 m with the flown clamps. So with fair
limits PID may hold height in the lab too; its struggle would then be swing and drift, not a sink.
Remaining asymmetry with zhd: MRAC's `u_ad` still bypasses the clamp. Say so in the demo write-up.

## 2. What happened at each switch-on on 10-07 (operator ask 2)

Pages: `docs/flights/plots/2026-10-07-exp{2,4,5}*.review.html`, section "MRAC injection switches" (4 s bins,
+-10 s plot). Measured from the WiFi logs:

| flight | before switch | first 4 s on | 4-8 s on | later |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| exp5 z (m), des 1.06 | 0.58 | 0.64 | 1.02 | 0.95-1.09 |
| exp5 pitch sd (deg) | 1.87 | 1.52 | 3.26 | 4.63 |
| exp5 corr(u_ad, pitch rate) | -0.23 | -0.22 | +0.43 | +0.73 |
| exp5 motor 2 at rail | 4 % | 3 % | 21 % | 32 % |
| exp4 pitch sd (deg) | 2.00 | 3.45 | 7.95 | off at 35.1 s, 8.83 |
| exp4 motor 2 at rail | 18 % | 43 % | 46 % | 38 % |

Reading (PROPOSED):
- MRAC fixes the sink in 4-8 s. Part of that is the clamp bypass (section 1).
- For the first ~4 s it damps (corr < 0, pitch sd drops in exp5). Then `u_ad` turns in phase with the rate
  (corr > 0): it pushes the pendulum, the swing grows 2-4x at about the same frequency.
- The growth tracks motor 2 at its 4000 rail. A pegged motor cannot add the correcting torque, so the loop
  loses authority on one side exactly when the swing needs it.
- Switching MRAC off (exp4, 35.1 s) did not stop the swing before landing: the stored pendulum energy stays.

What this means for the next flight:
1. Thrust headroom first: fresh pack, rope under the CG (measured offset +1..2 cm x, -6..7 cm y), check motor 2
   (its RPM channel also reads 0.31-0.39 of the others: hardware lead).
2. of1 off (presets 5/6): on the bench the of1 rotation leak (k ~ -0.6) is what grows the MRAC swing.
3. Abort on the signature: pitch swing growing over 3 cycles, or motor 2 pegged.
4. If it still grows with 1-3 fixed: firmware options below.

## 4. Firmware options if the swing still grows (operator decides; build only, default = flown)

Each one behind a flag, off by default, one Keil preset to switch it on:
- raise the `omega_u` low-pass (4 rad/s pitch, 5 rad/s roll today) to cut the lag that turns `u_ad` in phase;
- a rate-damping term on `u_ad` (subtract k * rate) so adaptation cannot push with the motion;
- a notch at the pendulum band (0.7-1.2 Hz; estimate 0.76 Hz for L ~ 0.43 m) on the adaptation input;
- sigma or e-modification leakage so the weights cannot wind up while the swing grows;
- a cap on `u_ad` so it cannot drive a motor into the rail.

The CEO builds one, checks it on the bench model, and the operator flashes it.
