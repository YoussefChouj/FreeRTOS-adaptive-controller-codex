R4 overnight report (2026-10-12): drift and live camera fusion

Goal: docs/lab/overnight-goal-2026-10-11.md. The drone was not connected: nothing was flashed, armed or captured. Every number is measured from the R4 logs unless it says computed, PROPOSED or UNVERIFIED.

Bottom line

Question Answer
Is drift solved? In replay, yes. Camera fixes stop the error from growing: on the 10 flights with good truth, EKF 30-146 cm rms becomes 5.1-13.6 cm rms (0.3 s latency, replay). Hover drift rate on b17: 0.29 to 0.03 cm/s.
Is the 10 cm / 60 s budget met? At 0.15 s latency and 20 fixes per second: yes, on all 4 flights (replay). Live tracker replay with the retuned firmware rule (gain 0.9, slew 10 cm, spread) and 20 Hz fixes (all PROPOSED), 1 s-mean 60 s drift p90: b17 hover 3.5, b13c 7.3, b5 7.4, b16 traj 7.4 cm (30 Hz: 3.0 / 6.3 / 6.3 / 6.5). At 10 Hz b16 missed (12.1). At 0.3 s latency only the hover passes (moving flights 11.3-12.3 at 20 Hz, 10.3-11.3 at 30 Hz). So two link gates are required: latency 0.15 s or less, and 20 or more fixes received per second. The real firmware C code, run on the same fixes, gives the same result within 0.5 cm (b16 7.9).
Can a small NN fix drift with the camera off? No. Best 34 cm (b15, in the training pool), best held-out 47 cm (b7); the b17 hold-out got worse (19 to 28 cm). The camera is required.
Lab tomorrow? Go, as a fusion bring-up day (run sheet R5). Dense trajectory presets and load scenarios only after the closed-loop hover gate passes, the measured latency is 0.15 s or less and the drone receives 20 or more fixes per second; start the dense presets at moderate speed (b16 runs at 38-40 cm/s were the worst, PROPOSED).

Think of the EKF as a walker counting steps with closed eyes, and the camera as a glance at a landmark twenty times a second. The glance stops the wandering. What is left is that each glance shows where you were 0.3 s ago.

Caveats that limit the claim: - Circular: the camera is both the fix source and the truth. - Open loop: these are replays; firmware mode 2 is built and host-tested but not flashed or flown. - Bad tracks defeat fusion: b8, b10 and b12 have coral-track jumps; fused there 70-92 cm rms (table B).

Truth height check: is the per-flight velocity scale (truth = 0.65 to 1.22 × EKF) an artefact of the camera truth using the wrong height plane? An independent height from the ring's pixel size follows the telemetry height (r 0.43 to 0.97) with a near-constant offset of +0.22 to +0.33 m on every flight. On hover b17 (lowest scale, 0.66), the ball-model height is 1.147 m against 1.149 m telemetry. Offset and scale do not move together (b5 0.22 m / 0.65, b2 0.33 m / 0.93), so the scale error is in the EKF flow velocity, not in the truth.

More data (UTIL, Toronto): 78 Crazyflie 2.1 flights with the same sensors (BMI088 + PMW3901 flow + ToF) and Vicon truth. Verdict (second pass): still no camera-off model at 10 cm. The best model (UTIL-pretrained TCN, only its output layer fine-tuned on our flights) gives b17 15.7 / b7 47.2 / b10 260 cm: better than the EKF on every held-out flight and better than pass 1, but 4-25x over budget on the moving flights. Even a well-calibrated Crazyflie flow deck drifts tens of cm per minute, so 10 cm per 60 s without an absolute reference is beyond this sensor class. Our error is a per-flight flow scale (truth ≈ 0.65-0.95 × EKF velocity) that none of our inputs reveal. What could move it: log PMW3901 surface quality and shutter at full rate, raw 100+ Hz IMU, and many more flights. The camera-on fusion (section 4, 3.5-7.9 cm) stays the path to the 10 cm budget.

4. Live camera fusion (built, replay-tested, not flashed)

Firmware EXT_POS mode 2 (e179e95f, retuned in 18c7d964): - Off at boot; Cmd 0xFF value 2 turns it on. - Each 10 Hz camera fix moves a target offset c: gain 0.9, at most 10 cm per fix (slew), 40 cm gate, re-anchor after 3 rejects, 32×50 ms history ring for the delay. All three values are PROPOSED (replay-chosen). - Spread (18c7d964): the applied offset a follows c at most 0.5 cm per 5 ms tick (1 m/s, 20 ticks for a full 10 cm step) and only that step is added to earth_x/y. Without it a 10 cm step lands in one tick, and the locx/locy PID has its D term on the error (Kd 4.0, pid.c:36): a 40 cm/s kick in the velocity setpoint. The ring stores earth - a (the raw EKF path); Reset_World_Origin zeroes a and c. - Host gcc test test_ext_pos.c passes (new checks: c step at most slew, earth step at most slew/20 per tick, a reaches c in 20 ticks). Keil build 0 errors; the first try failed writing OBJ/*.o ("Invalid argument", Windows Defender holding the files), the retry passed. Defender stayed on. - Fixed (test only): test_ekf_of_shadow.c test_reset_bias asserted the 10-03 bias freeze (q_bof 0), but 41c9dea2 (10-07) learns the bias again (q_bof 1e-5). It now checks a slow learn from 0; 8/8 pass. No firmware change. - Reset_World_Origin zeroes c; sysid start (send_data.c case 6) zeroes earth, so neither may run in mode 2. - Design deviation: this is the offset rule that the replay validated, not the delayed EKF state update in the design doc. The EKF itself (s_ekf_of) still dead-reckons; only the position the controller uses is corrected.

Ground side: - live_tracker (6e6e0759): coral tracker, jump gate (PROPOSED), 10 Hz fix scheduler stamped with capture time, scrcpy window source, and a UDP sender into stream_log --relay-port. - Preset drift_rc_extpos (d629deb1): priced, not applied. 85800 of 87552 B/s measured on the flashed ELF; 86300 computed for the mode-2 layout.

  • Firmware can take 20-30 Hz (read from the code, not bench-tested): the ground-station command queue is 16 deep and drained every 10 ms (USER/main.c:350); Cmd_ExtPos writes a single-slot mailbox and Ext_Pos_Tick (TASK/StabilizerTask.c:872) uses the newest fix each 5 ms tick. A burst loses old fixes, not new ones. Frames have no ACK, so the received rate must be measured on the bench (run sheet step 1: growth of g_ext_pos.n_ok + n_rej + n_old).
  • Link load: one fix frame is 9 bytes, so 20 Hz is 180 B/s and 30 Hz 270 B/s before UDP/WiFi overhead (computed).
  • At 0.3 s latency no rate saves the moving flights (10.3-12.3 cm), so latency stays the first gate.
  • Delay setting off by 50 ms (--delay-err, 20 Hz, 0.15 s link), 1 s-d60 p90 / rms cm: b16 7.4 becomes 6.8 / 6.5 (+50 ms) and 9.5 / 7.0 (-50 ms); b13c 7.3 becomes 9.4 / 7.7 (+50 ms) and 7.4 / 7.1 (-50 ms). Still inside the budget, with 0.5-0.6 cm left at the worst case, so the latency event must pin --delay-ms to within 50 ms (PROPOSED).
