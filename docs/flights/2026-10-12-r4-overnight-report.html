R4 overnight report (2026-10-12): drift and live camera fusion

Goal: docs/lab/overnight-goal-2026-10-11.md. The drone was not connected: nothing was flashed, armed or captured. Every number is measured from the R4 logs unless it says computed, PROPOSED or UNVERIFIED.

Bottom line

Question Answer
Is drift solved? In replay, yes. Camera fixes stop the error from growing: on the 10 flights with good truth, EKF 30-146 cm rms becomes 5.1-13.6 cm rms (0.3 s latency, replay). Hover drift rate on b17: 0.29 to 0.03 cm/s.
Is the 10 cm / 60 s budget met? Hover only. b17 1 s-mean 60 s drift p90: 9.2 cm at 0.15 s latency, 10.3 at 0.3 s. Moving flights: 16-27 cm. The rest is latency.
Can a small NN fix drift with the camera off? No. Best 34 cm (b15, in the training pool), best held-out 47 cm (b7); the b17 hold-out got worse (19 to 28 cm). The camera is required.
Lab tomorrow? Go, as a fusion bring-up day (run sheet R5). Dense trajectory presets and load scenarios only after the closed-loop hover gate passes.

Think of the EKF as a walker counting steps with closed eyes, and the camera as a glance at a landmark ten times a second. The glance stops the wandering. What is left is that each glance shows where you were 0.3 s ago.

Caveats that limit the claim: - Circular: the camera is both the fix source and the truth. - Open loop: these are replays; firmware mode 2 is built and host-tested but not flashed or flown. - Bad tracks defeat fusion: b8, b10 and b12 have coral-track jumps; fused there 70-92 cm rms (table B).

3. Small NN with the camera off (STM32F4-sized)

Literature check (abstracts verified on arXiv):

AirIO 2501.15659 UAV inertial odometry, body-frame input + attitude encoding closest; our design follows it
AI-IMU 1904.06064 adapts the Kalman noise cars (KITTI)
Gyro denoise 2002.10718 gyro correction attitude only (EuRoC)

None has weights for a BMI088 at 100 Hz plus optical flow, so nothing was downloaded; we trained our own.

Design (video_truth nn4): - Target: body-frame velocity residual, the derivative of (truth − EKF), rotated into the body frame. - Yaw sign chosen per flight from the EKF's own body velocity (no truth needed, so usable on board). - Input: 17 channels, 10 block means over a 1 s causal window, so 170 features at 10 Hz. - The prediction is integrated onto the EKF position and scored like the other columns.

4. Live camera fusion (built, replay-tested, not flashed)

Firmware EXT_POS mode 2 (e179e95f): - Off at boot; Cmd 0xFF value 2 turns it on. - Each 10 Hz camera fix nudges an offset c on earth_x/y: gain 0.1, slew at most 3 cm per step, 40 cm gate, re-anchor after 3 rejects, 32×50 ms history ring for the delay. - Host gcc test passes; Keil build 0 errors (4 old warnings). - Reset_World_Origin zeroes c; sysid start (send_data.c case 6) zeroes earth, so neither may run in mode 2. - Design deviation: this is the offset rule that the replay validated, not the delayed EKF state update in the design doc. The EKF itself (s_ekf_of) still dead-reckons; only the position the controller uses is corrected.

Ground side: - live_tracker (6e6e0759): coral tracker, jump gate (PROPOSED), 10 Hz fix scheduler stamped with capture time, scrcpy window source, and a UDP sender into stream_log --relay-port. - Preset drift_rc_extpos (d629deb1): priced, not applied. 85800 of 87552 B/s measured on the flashed ELF; 86300 computed for the mode-2 layout.
