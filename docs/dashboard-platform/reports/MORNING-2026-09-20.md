### Known gaps: fixed on 09-20 (service tests: 104 passed)

- **Arm gate fails closed:** `is_disarmed()` returns False when no arm sample has arrived or the last one is older than 2 s. The 0x16 gate then rejects with "arm state unknown".
- **Command timeout:** a command with no firmware result after 1.0 s (`COMMAND_TIMEOUT_NS`) becomes `timed_out` and is logged once in `/api/faults`.
- **Replay feeds the live bus:** `POST /replay/<session_id>/play` pushes stored telemetry through the live ingest path. It is not stored again, it doesn't confirm commands, and it sends nothing to the drone. Live test: 1177 records replayed.
- **SQLite:** every access now goes through one `RLock`.
