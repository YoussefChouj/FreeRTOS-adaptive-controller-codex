# Firmware Flight-Safety Audit Report

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Target:** STM32F407 FreeRTOS Adaptive Flight Controller Firmware  
**Scope:** `TASK/`, `API/`, `BSP/`, `USER/` (read-only audit, no source changes)  
**Compiler Target:** Keil ARMCC V5.06 (C89 / ARM Embedded ABI)  

---

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive, read-only flight-safety audit was conducted on the core firmware codebase prior to flight testing. The audit focused on six critical areas: arming/disarming and failsafes, control paths (PID, MRAC, motor mixing), concurrency and ISR safety, sensor drivers and health monitoring, memory safety in parsers, and ground-station telemetry/command execution paths.

A total of **37 verified findings** were identified:
- **15 P0 Findings (Critical / Flight-Fatal):** Defects that can directly induce flyaways, in-flight motor shutoffs, uncontrolled flips, or system freezes during flight.
- **16 P1 Findings (Major / Degraded Flight):** Flaws that corrupt control margins, destroy anti-windup or calibration guarantees, cause telemetry packet loss, or violate RTOS invariants.
- **6 P2 Findings (Hygiene / Fidelity):** Code unreachable or distorted, unit scale mismatches in shadow estimators, and dead-loop compute spikes.

  - **P0 Finding 7 (MRAC pitch sign):** FALSE-POSITIVE. Auditor claimed pitch injection sign was inverted because mixer uses `+u_gyroy` on front motors. In reality, `Ctrler.gyroyPID.FB = -Gyro_Y_Real` is already negated ($x = -q$). Positive physical pitch rate produces negative $x$, leading to positive adaptive output $u_{ad} > 0$. Injected into `u_gyroy = -(U + mrac_pitch)`, this drives front motors down and rear motors up, opposing the disturbance and producing positive acceleration in $x$ ($\dot{x} = -\dot{q} > 0$). Effective plant gain $B$ is strictly positive ($B > 0$). Applying auditor's suggested patch would create an actual positive-feedback sign inversion and flip the drone.
